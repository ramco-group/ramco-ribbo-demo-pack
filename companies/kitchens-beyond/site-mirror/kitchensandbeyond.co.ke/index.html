Our Story

Over 13 Years of
Italian Opulence
in Kenya

Kitchens & Beyond is Kenya's premier one-stop shop for luxury interior solutions — kitchens, wardrobes, sanitaryware, tiles, vanities and 3D interior design. Part of the Ramco Group, with roots going back to 1948.

We began with a single ambition: to introduce modular kitchens to the Kenyan market through our founding partnership with Italian brand Snaidero. That success prompted a broader vision — to become a genuine one-stop shop for interior solutions.

Featured Brand

Snaidero
Italian Kitchens

Snaidero is the Italian kitchen brand that started it all for Kitchens & Beyond. With over 70 years of craftsmanship, Snaidero brings the true taste of Italian opulence — precision engineering meets timeless design.

Each kitchen is bespoke, modular, and built to the exact specifications of your space. Our in-house design team handles the entire journey from first sketch to final installation.

  • ✓ Made-to-measure modular design
  • ✓ Minimum 10-year product warranty
  • ✓ 3D visualisation before you commit
